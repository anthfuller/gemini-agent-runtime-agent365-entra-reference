# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participants must maintain a professional, respectful, inclusive, and security-conscious project environment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical decisions constructively and with evidence.
- Respect differing experience levels and perspectives.
- Keep reviews focused on the contribution.
- Protect confidential information, credentials, and personal data.
- Correct mistakes transparently and without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, credential exposure, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this policy and may restrict participation when necessary. Security-sensitive matters must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
